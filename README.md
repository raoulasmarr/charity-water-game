# Pipe Connect

Pipe Connect is a responsive browser game inspired by charity: water's mission to help bring clean and safe drinking water to communities around the world.

Play it here: https://raoulasmarr.github.io/charity-water-game/

## About the Game

The goal is to connect a water source to a village by rotating pipe pieces. Each completed pipeline represents clean water reaching another village. The game uses hopeful, optimistic messaging and charity: water-inspired colors, including bright yellow, teal, clean whites, soft grays, and animated blue water.

## How to Play

1. Press **Start Game**.
2. Click or tap pipe tiles to rotate them 90 degrees clockwise.
3. Connect the water source on the left to the village on the right.
4. Complete all 10 levels, which grow from simple 4x4 puzzles to larger, more complex layouts.
5. When the path is complete, water flows through the connected pipes and the village lights up.

There is no timer. The game tracks moves and score, but the focus is on solving each level at your own pace.

## About charity: water

charity: water is a nonprofit organization focused on bringing clean and safe water to people around the world. This game is a small educational project built to reflect that mission in a positive, interactive way.

Learn more at https://www.charitywater.org
